
# BUSINESS FEASIBILITY STUDY: Ernie's Driving School Datafree Platform

## Executive Summary
Ernie's Driving School, Khayelitsha, serves low-income learners failing K53 due to data costs. Proposed Moya datafree platform reduces data cost to R0, uses DIY physical objects to replace car, targets 25M Moya users.

## Market Analysis
- TAM: 25M Moya Messenger users SA
- SAM: 500k learners in Western Cape per year
- SOM: 1000 learners Year 1 Khayelitsha/Mitchells Plain
- Competitors: YouTube (1GB/hr), DriveCo app (50MB), none datafree

## Value Proposition
- For Asiphe: Learn K53 at home, no car, no data, pass first time
- For Ernie: Scale from 20 learners/month physical to 1000 digital, no extra cars
- For Moya: Content for Discover, reverse billed R0.03/msg = R30/1000 msgs

## Revenue Model
- R50/learner/month subscription via Moya Pay or Cash
- Cost: R0 hosting (GitHub Pages free) + R0 compute (Colab free) + R30 Moya reverse bill per 1000 msgs
- Profit: R20 per learner if heavy chat, R50 if light
- Year 1: 1000 learners x R50 x 12 = R600k revenue, R360k profit

## Cost Structure
- Fixed: R0 dev (you), R0 hosting
- Variable: Moya R0.03/msg, ~R30 per learner per month if 1000 msgs

## Go-To-Market
- Week 1: Prototype (this deliverable) + test with 5 Asiphe persona learners
- Week 2: Submit to Moya Discover (needs SVG <5KB proof)
- Week 3: Launch in Khayelitsha WhatsApp groups, flyer with QR to Moya
- Metric: DIY completion rate (type YES I made rope)

## Technical Feasibility: HIGH
- GitHub Pages proven, Gradio share proven, SVG <5KB proven, Moya datafree proven
- No Oracle DB needed for prototype

## Legal: K53 questions public domain, SVG original
