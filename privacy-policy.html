Privacy Policy (POPIA Compliance)

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), Ernie's Driving School is committed to protecting your personal data privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver our services, including name, email address, practice test results, and usage logs.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for student progress tracking, database syncing, service evaluation, and administrative communications.

3. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under POPIA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your personal information stored in our systems.

4. Information Officer Contact

For privacy queries or data removal requests, contact our designated Information Officer at privacy@erniesdrivingschool.co.za.
